Questions

    
The things people ask before the first box.

    

      

        Can I choose what's in the box?
        
Not the curries — there's one menu a day, and that's what keeps it fresh and cheap. You choose the basic box or the 450g chicken one, and you can tell us what to leave out, which we honour for every box after that without being reminded.

      

      

        Why only twenty boxes?
        
Because one kitchen cooks them all, the same morning, to the count of orders. Twenty is what fits in one run while the food is still hot. A month plan holds one of the twenty for you; day orders take whatever is left.

      

      

        What if I'm not there when the rider comes?
        
Name a place and a person on the form — a gate, a shop, a neighbour. The rider leaves it there and messages you. We don't take the box back for a missed delivery; it's still your box.

      

      

        How do I pay for a month every month?
        
A standing order at your own bank, set for the 1st. You control it and can stop it without asking us. If you'd rather not, we'll message you a reminder each month and you transfer manually — we don't keep card details, because this site has no way to store them.

      

      

        Can I stop?
        
Any time, with a message. Days you've paid for and haven't used stay yours — they roll into the next plan, or we transfer the balance back to the account you paid from.
